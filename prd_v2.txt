​1. Executive Summary & Vision​
​CostView 360 is a flagship construction cost intelligence platform designed to eliminate the fragmentation​
​between physical site operations and commercial financial tracking. By unifying budget tracking,​
​procurement, material inventory, labour productivity, and project progress into a single Command Center,​
​CostView 360 provides executives, project managers, and quantity surveyors with real-time, actionable​
​insights.​
​This PRD details the 10X expansion of the platform's capabilities, breaking down the application into​
​high-fidelity functional requirements, complex multi-stakeholder workflows, and a scalable, robust​
​technical architecture leveraging modern web and cloud technologies.​

​3. Deep Dive: Site Operations Module​
​The Site Operations module is the execution engine of the project, tracking the day-to-day physical and​
​financial reality.​

​3.1 Budget Management​
​●​ ​Bill of Quantities (BOQ) Integration:​​Live parsing​​of uploaded BOQ data. Support for​
​multi-level hierarchical cost codes.​
​●​ ​Live Cost Control:​​Real-time variance calculation​​tracking Approved Budget vs. Committed Cost​
​(POs) vs. Actual Cost (Paid).​
​●​ ​Predictive Forecasting:​​Trend analysis using historical​​burn rates to project Estimate at​
​Completion (EAC) and Estimate to Complete (ETC).​
​●​ ​Final Account Reconciliations:​​Automated aggregation​​of variations, prime cost sums, and​
​provisional sums into a final closing ledger.​

​3.2 Procurement Lifecycle​
​●​ ​Requisitions Workflow:​​Auto-trigger requisitions based​​on material reorder levels.​
​●​ ​Supplier Enquiry Board:​​Side-by-side bid comparisons​​tracking lead times, payment terms, and​
​historical supplier quality scores.​
​●​ ​Three-Way Financial Match:​​Automated validation between​​the Purchase Order (PO), Goods​
​Received Note (GRN), and Supplier Invoice. Flagging discrepancies (e.g., 190 bags delivered vs​
​200 bags invoiced).​

​●​ ​Supplier Leaderboards:​​Automated scoring based on On-Time Delivery (OTD), Quality Pass​
​Rates, and Price Competitiveness.​

​3.3 Materials & Inventory​
​●​ ​Live Stock Gauges:​​Visual tracking of available, reserved,​​and consumed stock levels per SKU.​
​●​ ​Site-to-Site Transfers:​​Kanban-style tracking of material​​movement across multi-location​
​projects.​
​●​ ​Variance Logging:​​Dedicated workflows for logging​​damaged goods, over-issues, and​
​reconciliation discrepancies.​

​3.4 Labour & Productivity​
​●​ ​Attendance Matrix:​​Weekly grid inputs for workers,​​crews, and trades. Integration with biometric​
​site scanners (future-proofing).​
​●​ ​Automated Payroll Calculation:​​Basic pay, overtime​​multipliers, and automated deduction​
​processing.​
​●​ ​Crew Productivity Scoring:​​Cross-referencing BOQ output​​metrics against labour hours to​
​calculate real-time efficiency metrics (e.g., blocks laid per man-hour).​

​3.5 Progress, Quality & Safety​
​●​ ​Digital Site Diary:​​Time-stamped daily logs cross-referenced​​with local weather API data and​
​workforce headcount.​
​●​ ​Snagging & NCRs:​​Visual Kanban board for Non-Conformance​​Reports (NCRs), tracking issues​
​from discovery to remediation and sign-off.​
​●​ ​Safety Scorecards:​​Hero-metric displays for "Days​​Without Lost-Time Incident" and near-miss​
​logs.​

​4. Deep Dive: Commercial & Developer Mode​
​This module adapts to the corporate structure of the user, splitting into Contractor and Developer​
​contexts.​

​4.1 Contractor Commercial Operations​
​●​ ​Tender & Estimating:​​Pre-construction cost build-ups,​​preliminary pricing, and margin​
​application.​
​●​ ​Valuation & Certification:​​Generation of interim payment​​applications to the client based on​
​verified physical progress.​

​●​ ​Claims & Entitlements:​​Documenting loss and expense, linking site diary delays (weather, client​
​changes) directly to Extension of Time (EOT) requests.​

​4.2 Developer / Asset Owner Operations​
​●​ ​Feasibility & Acquisition:​​Land cost tracking, ROI​​projections, and yield analyses.​
​●​ ​Project Finance & Capital Stack:​​Tracking equity drawdown,​​development loans, and interest​
​capitalization.​
​●​ ​Sales & Receivables:​​Unit pricing, buyer ledgers,​​payment schedules, and collection status​
​tracking.​

​5. Technical Architecture & Backend Flow​
​The system requires a highly scalable, real-time architecture capable of processing thousands of financial​
​transactions, material movements, and user events securely.​

​5.1 Core Technology Stack​
​●​ ​Frontend Web Application:​​Built using​​Next.js​,​​React​,​​and​​TypeScript​. Styled comprehensively​
​with​​Tailwind CSS​​to replicate the high-fidelity UI​​specifications.​
​●​ ​Backend Services & API:​​Developed in​​Python​​(using​​FastAPI or Django), enabling rapid data​
​processing, complex mathematical cost aggregations, and integration with AI libraries (e.g.,​
​TensorFlow) for predictive forecasting.​
​●​ ​Primary Database (Relational):​​Hosted on​​Supabase​​(PostgreSQL)​​to ensure ACID​
​compliance, strict relational integrity for financial ledgers, and Row Level Security (RLS).​
​●​ ​Real-Time Event Sync:​​Powered by​​Firebase​​Realtime​​Database and Cloud Messaging to push​
​instant updates to the Notification Bell, Snag Kanban boards, and collaborative Site Diaries.​

​5.3 Critical Backend Workflows​
​Flow 1: The Three-Way Match (Invoice Approval)​
​1.​ ​Trigger:​​Supplier submits an Invoice payload via API​​or manual entry.​
​2.​ ​Python Backend Processing:​​The API fetches the corresponding​​Purchase Order and GRN​
​records from Supabase.​
​3.​ ​Validation Engine:​​The system compares:​
​○​ ​PO.quantity vs GRN.quantity_received vs Invoice.billed_quantity​
​○​ ​PO.agreed_rate vs Invoice.unit_rate​
​4.​ ​Outcome Routing:​
​○​ ​If Perfect Match:​​Update Invoice status to 'Matched',​​trigger Webhook to Accounting (e.g.,​
​QuickBooks), and push Firebase notification to Finance Officer.​
​○​ ​If Discrepancy:​​Update status to 'Discrepancy', lock​​payment release, and push Firebase​
​alert to Procurement Officer outlining the exact variance.​
​Flow 2: Automated AI Cost Forecasting​
​1.​ ​Trigger:​​Weekly CRON job or manual refresh.​

​2.​ ​Data Aggregation:​​Python backend pulls time-series data of material consumption rates, labour​
​efficiency scores, and current weather delays.​
​3.​ ​TensorFlow Model Execution:​​Feeds historical baseline​​data through the predictive model to​
​calculate potential cost overruns for ongoing BOQ items.​
​4.​ ​Insight Generation:​​Writes records to the ai_insights​​table, feeding the Reports Studio and​
​highlighting risks in the Executive Command Center.​

​6. Integrations & Webhooks​
​●​ ​Accounting & ERP:​​Native two-way sync with QuickBooks​​and Xero for Accounts Payable​
​(Invoices) and Accounts Receivable (Certifications).​
​●​ ​Document Storage:​​Automated PDF generation for POs​​and Reports, syncing directly to Google​
​Drive / SharePoint via OAuth2 tokens.​
​●​ ​Communication:​​Email via SendGrid and WhatsApp Business​​API integrations for urgent​
​supplier notifications and internal approval requests.​

​8. Implementation & Rollout Strategy​
​To mitigate risk and ensure maximum user adoption, the platform will be developed and released in​
​strategic phases.​
​●​ ​Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1-3):​​Supabase schema​​design, Next.js UI scaffolding,​
​Authentication, Core BOQ import, and basic Budget tracking.​
​●​ ​Phase 2: Operations & Real-Time (Months 4-6):​​Procurement​​workflows, Firebase real-time​
​notifications, Materials inventory, and Labour logs.​
​●​ ​Phase 3: Commercial & Integrations (Months 7-8):​​Developer/Contractor​​toggles, QuickBooks​
​integration, and Document generation.​
​●​ ​Phase 4: Intelligence (Months 9-10):​​Python-based​​predictive analytics, Reports Studio, and​
​advanced Supplier Performance algorithms.​
